About Cuidado Casa by KC

Thoughtful Care, Nature & Memorable Experiences

Cuidado Casa by KC is a hospitality destination in Horana, Sri Lanka, built around our belief that every guest deserves genuine care, comfort, and a warm welcome.

Our vision is to bring together accommodation, dining, nature, and authentic Sri Lankan experiences in one welcoming place. With a focus on personal service and meaningful experiences, we aim to create a place where guests can relax, connect with local culture, and make lasting memories.

Warm Personal Hospitality

Tailored attention for solo travelers, couples, families, and culture enthusiasts.

Serene Natural Setting in Horana

Comfortable rooms with greenery, balcony, and pool/garden views.

Cultural Experience Packages

Home-cooked meals, cooking lessons, Sinhala phrases, craft workshops, and local tours.

Exclusive Cultural Package

THE SRI LANKAN SOUL JOURNEY

“Discover our island. Share your culture. Leave a little piece of yourself behind.”

A four-day, three-night cultural experience based at Cuidado Casa by KC in Horana. Enjoy Sri Lankan food, learn simple Sinhala phrases, share traditions, explore nature, join a cooking lesson, and create a coconut-shell keepsake.

Duration: 4 Days / 3 Nights
Shared Room Price: LKR 45,000 / person
Private Room Option: LKR 60,000 – 65,000 / person
Group Size: Max 8 Guests
